# Incident Oversight Workflow

Status date: 2026-08-09

## Intake

The reviewer creates a contractor-linked incident by default, or a project/GC incident when no subcontractor applies. Required intake is project, incident date/time, factual description, category, and optional location/activity/affected-work scope.

## Contractor Information

Contractor reports, photos, witness statements, corrective-action documentation, and revised submissions are attached as preserved source records. Contractor investigation status, contractor-reported classification, findings, and corrective actions remain contractor-provided information.

## GC / Project Review

The reviewer creates a separate GC/project review that evaluates project exposure, related plan/procedure concerns, adequacy from the project perspective, additional information needs, and management-review need. This review does not replace the contractor investigation.

## Recommendations and Decisions

Project recommendations are editable and human-controlled. Project safety decisions require human confirmation and preserve the incident origin, decision text, scope, effective date, rationale, status, and decision-maker.

## Follow-Up and Closure

The reviewer records follow-up verification and may close the oversight record when project-level follow-up is complete. Closure preserves closure note, project outcome, unresolved contractor-level items, reviewer, and date. Reopening preserves the original closure event and records the reopen reason.

## Links

Incidents may link to relevant plan findings and observations. Links are factual relationships for review context and do not prove causation.

## Exclusions

The workflow does not implement OSHA 300/301/300A, workers compensation, insurance claims, legal liability scoring, automatic recordability, automatic citation exposure, automatic contractor suspension, reporting, portals, or historical scoring.
