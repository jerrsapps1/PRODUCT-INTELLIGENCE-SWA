# Field Observation Workflow

Status date: 2026-08-09

## Entry

The reviewer selects a project, optionally selects a contractor engagement, enters the observation text, and may add location, activity, category, classification, follow-up-needed state, and photos. Project-level/general observations are valid without a contractor.

## Preservation

The original observation text is preserved as entered. Later classification, category, summaries, AI suggestions, reviewer notes, photos, reference links, plan-finding links, and follow-up changes are separate records or fields.

## Enrichment

The reviewer may run observation suggestions after save. Suggestions may classify the observation as positive, neutral/informational, concern, corrected in field, or follow-up required. Suggestions may also propose category, activity, summary, follow-up state, and grounded source references when existing source chunks are available.

## Review

The reviewer may edit or reject suggestions. Rejected suggestions are not silently restored by later processing. Concern observations do not become OSHA violations, incidents, citations, corrective actions, discipline records, or performance ratings.

## Follow-Up

Follow-up state is none, needed, or verified/closed. Corrected-in-field observations are supported without requiring incident escalation.

## History

Observation lists are chronological within the project and support filters by contractor engagement, classification, category, follow-up state, and date range. Contractor workspace views may show relevant contractor observations as history, but not scoring.
